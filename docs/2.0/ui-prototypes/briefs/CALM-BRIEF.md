# Round 127 to 129: Mac at Center, calm

The owner on 113-a, Mac at Center, gallery number 204: "We like 204 - but it looks very busy - we love the focus on details - but there is so many points - also keep working with the lid closed should be something in settings not others".

Start from a copy of `p/113-a.html`. Keep its idea: the Mac as one gauge in the middle, memory by holder inside and disk as the outer track, every part on a side with a home on the ring, Fix Everything and the deck under it. Keep every fact rule in `briefs/REAL-BRIEF.md`.

## What is wrong today

At Busy the Performance tab shows about 50 points at once:
- about 35 rows in eight groups on the two sides
- a badge on many rows
- seven fix cards with two numbers and a button each
- a CPU and network line
- a footer with the lid checkbox and two lines of rule text

Each one is right, but together they are too many to read in a glance.

## What the owner loves

The focus on details:
- each part has a home on the ring
- hovering a row ties it to its slice
- a fix lights its parts on both sides
- the center shows the Mac after a fix

Keep all of that. It becomes what you see when you point at something, not what you see at rest.

## Calm rules for every page

- **At rest, at most 15 points show.** A point is a row, a fix card, a number with its own label, or a badge. Count them at Busy and write the count in the stage caption.
- **No badge at rest.** A badge shows only on a row you point at, or on a row that needs you, such as an agent that asks.
- **One number per row at most.** A second number, a state word and the owner name show in the detail view.
- **The deck shows the first fix in full.** The rest are one line, "and 6 more fixes · 3.4 GB memory, 31.1 GB disk", which opens the list.
- **Peak may hold more parts, but never more points at rest.** It folds harder.
- **The lid setting is not on Performance.** "Keep working with the lid closed" lives only in Settings, in the This Mac card, as on 60-a. Remove it from every other tab and footer.
- **The rest of the room rules from round 60 still hold.** Padding, gaps, 6 px around labels, 12 px text and nothing cut.

## Three directions

- **127-a: Fewer, bigger.** Each side shows its groups as one line each, with a name and a total. A group opens in place on hover or click, to its biggest five. The ring keeps every slice. Hovering a slice opens its group and lights its row.
- **128-a: The ring chooses.** At rest, the left side lists what holds memory and the right side shows the detail of whatever you point at: a part, a slice or a fix. Pointing at the outer disk track turns the left side to disk. One side is always details, so the focus on details stays in full.
- **129-a: Quiet until asked.** At rest, only the ring, its one sentence, Fix Everything and the first fix show, with the three biggest holders of memory and of disk named around the ring. Everything else fades in when the pointer enters a side, and fades out when it leaves.

Each page keeps Quiet, Busy and Peak, and its other tabs as 113-a has them. The one change outside Performance is that the lid setting moves to Settings.
