What the grid gives up at Peak
  • Bigger cells mean fewer tiles in view. The field scrolls, and the line under it counts the tiles below that need you.
  • Ready tiles sort to the top. A tile that asks you grows in place, so some neighbours glide to new places.
  • Small tiles draw no nodes. They show one line of counts, and the hover card has the rest.
  • A tall tile names a server by its port and counts its tabs. The framework and the connections show on big tiles and in the card.
  • A big tile draws five holding rows and a tall one three. The rest are counted, and the card lists them.
  • Five columns often leave the last rows short. A legend tile fills those cells, so the grid shows no hole.
  • The leftovers line shows the biggest few. Its card lists every one.
  • Tiles below the fold draw no ripples until you scroll to them.
